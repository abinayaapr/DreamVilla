Estimation

Fifteen calculators, no spreadsheet

Work out what to order before you ring the supplier. Concrete uses the standard 1.54 dry-volume allowance and real nominal mixes; steel uses the d²/162 rule; the staircase checks 2R+T for comfort.

  • Construction cost, budget planner and a stage-wise split of where it goes
  • Concrete (M7.5–M25), bricks and blocks, steel, tiles, flooring, paint
  • Water tank sized to occupants, compound wall, parking, staircase
  • EMI with a year-by-year amortisation chart, loan eligibility, interest

Now on Android too

The same app, rebuilt natively for Android rather than wrapped in a web view — the same sixteen modules, the same lakh-and-crore money, the same records format. A backup exported on one platform restores on the other.

Kotlin and Compose

Built with Compose Multiplatform on Android 8.0 and up. Material 3 components, the system light and dark theme, and edge-to-edge layout throughout.

Backup where you choose

A daily archive into any folder you pick — Google Drive included — keeping the three most recent. Records also ride Android's own account backup, so a new phone starts where the old one left off.

Reminders that arrive

Payment due dates, utility bills, site reminders, service dates and warranty expiries all surface as notifications on the morning they matter.

Not everything, yet

Thirteen of the fifteen calculators are there. Biometric lock, PDF reports and the map picker are still iOS-only — reports share as text on Android for now.

Privacy

Your records never leave your device.

There is no account to create and no server of ours to sign in to. Your deed scans, vendor numbers and what you paid for steel stay exactly where you put them — they move only when you export a backup or share something yourself. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which never receives any of your property data.

Stored locally, encrypted by the OS

Records live inside the app's private storage; photos, PDFs and voice notes sit alongside as files. Both platforms encrypt that storage at rest while the device is locked.

Ads, and nothing else

No analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking library. The only third party is Google AdMob, which serves the ads that keep the app free and never sees your houses, expenses, documents or contacts. The developer cannot read your data because the developer never receives it.

Your backup, your file

Export everything as a single .dreamvilla file, optionally with every photo embedded. Where it goes — Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Downloads — is entirely your call.

Locked if you want it (iOS)

An optional passcode is stored only as a salted hash — never the digits. Face ID or Touch ID unlocks it, and the app hides its contents whenever it goes to the background.

Where the project stands

Both apps are complete and running. These are the things they deliberately do not do yet.

The calculators are planning estimates, not engineering. They use industry thumb rules, and rates vary a lot by city and finish. Confirm quantities with a qualified engineer or architect before you order materials or build.

No live sync yet

One device, one copy — and no shared editing between your phone and someone else's. Moving to a new phone is a backup and a restore. Real sync is planned, and it will be opt-in.

No sharing with family

You can share individual contacts, documents and reports, but two people can't keep the same house up to date together. That's Phase 2.

Bills are typed, not scanned

You attach a photo of the bill and enter the amount yourself. OCR scanning and automatic categorisation are on the list, not in the app.

Voice notes aren't transcribed

Site diary recordings play back but aren't turned into text yet. Widgets, Siri Shortcuts and Live Activities are also still ahead.
