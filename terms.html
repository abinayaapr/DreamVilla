About Dream Villa

Dream Villa is an iOS app that lets you create a digital file for each property you own, recording everything from planning and construction through long-term maintenance. All your property records are stored locally on your device and do not require an account or internet connection. The app makes network requests for exactly two things: serving ads via Google AdMob in the free tier, and processing the optional Remove Ads subscription through Apple's StoreKit.

What You Can Do With the App

You may use Dream Villa for personal, non-commercial record-keeping of your own properties only. You may take photos, record notes, store documents, and organise maintenance schedules for houses, plots, or properties you own. You may export your data and back it up as described in the privacy policy. You may not distribute, sell, rent, modify, or create derivative works from the app itself or its contents.

The Free Plan and What a Subscription Adds

Dream Villa is free to use, within limits. On the free plan you may keep one house, and up to 20 records in each module of that house. Modules are counted separately, so contacts, expenses, documents, progress photos, diary entries, materials, maintenance records, utility bills, warranties and the rest each have their own allowance of 20.

Exporting a backup requires a subscription. Restoring from a backup does not, and never will — a backup file you already hold is always yours to load back in.

A subscription lifts every one of these limits, removes all advertising, and enables backup export.

Records you have already saved are never deleted because of a limit. If you exceed a limit — for example by restoring a backup made while subscribed — the extra records remain on your device untouched; the app simply does not display them, and will not let you add more, until you subscribe. Everything reappears the moment a subscription is active again.

The developer may change what the free plan includes in a future version. Any such change will be described in the release notes and reflected in these terms before it takes effect.

Estimation Calculators: Disclaimer

Dream Villa includes estimation calculators for construction costs, concrete, bricks, steel, tiles, paint, water tanks, compound walls, parking, staircases, EMI, loan eligibility, and interest. These calculators produce quick estimates based on industry thumb rules for planning purposes only.

  • These are NOT engineering, architectural, valuation, legal, tax, or financial advice.
  • Estimates must be verified with a qualified engineer or architect before you order materials or start construction.
  • Do not rely on these calculations alone for budget approvals, loan applications, or procurement decisions.
  • The accuracy and suitability of estimates depend on your local costs, labour availability, and site conditions, which vary by region.
  • The developer is not liable for inaccurate estimates, cost overruns, or any decisions made based on the calculator results.

Dream Villa also uses rewarded ads: short video ads you choose to watch in exchange for a specific action, such as exporting a report or adding an expense while on the free plan. You can remove both banner and rewarded ads at any time with the optional subscription described below.
